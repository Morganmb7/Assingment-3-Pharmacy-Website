THE BUSINESS PROBLEM

Scattered records. Manual checks. Missed warning signs.

Small pharmacies in the United States can struggle with fragmented inventory and operational data. Stock counts, supplier deliveries, expiration dates, and adjustments may be recorded in separate places, making it difficult to see what is happening across the pharmacy.

Staff must compare records and verify quantities manually before deciding what needs attention. Without a reliable, current view of inventory, problems can be discovered only after they affect daily operations.

▤

Stock Discrepancies

Recorded quantities may not match what is physically on the shelf. Finding the cause requires checking deliveries, adjustments, and stock movements across multiple records. Unresolved differences make purchasing decisions less reliable.

◷

Expiration and Overstock Risks

Manual expiration checks make it harder to consistently identify products approaching their expiration dates. Excess stock can tie up money and shelf space, while unused medications may become avoidable waste.

⇄

Supply and Availability Gaps

Low stock and delayed deliveries can be difficult to anticipate when supply activity is tracked separately. Teams may need to place urgent orders or spend additional time resolving medication availability issues.

THE PHARMAFLOW SOLUTION

One connected view for better inventory decisions.

PharmaFlow's proposed solution transforms manual tracking into a database-backed web application. Centralized records and an interactive dashboard would give pharmacy managers and inventory staff a shared place to monitor activity, investigate problems, and prioritize action.

◫

Monitor Key Indicators

Use dashboard KPIs, charts, and alerts to review stock levels, low-stock products, inventory value, and expiration risks. A consolidated overview helps staff identify which items require a closer look.

✓

Explain Inventory Variances

Compare physical counts with recorded quantities and review the related stock movements. Tracking receipts and adjustments together would help teams investigate discrepancies and keep records accurate.

↗

Plan Purchases with Evidence

Review inventory trends alongside supplier delivery performance to support reorder decisions. Identify recurring delays, prioritize products at risk of running out, and avoid buying excess stock.

FROM INSIGHT TO ACTION

Help pharmacy teams act before problems grow.

A low-stock indicator can prompt a reorder review. An expiry alert can bring attention to products that need action. An inventory variance can trigger a physical count and a review of recent movements. Supplier trends can help managers address recurring delivery issues.

The goal is to reduce time spent reconciling records, improve inventory accuracy, limit expiration-related waste, and support medication availability. Pharmacy staff verify the findings and approve the appropriate actions.
