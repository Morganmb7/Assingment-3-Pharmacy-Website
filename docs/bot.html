About the data.

The assistant searches a Botpress knowledge base built from a table of 15 medications from a sample US retail pharmacy, the same records used on our Inventory and Dashboard pages. For each medication it knows the name and strength, batch number, expiry date, quantity on shelf, supplier, wholesale cost, retail price (30–33% markup), therapeutic category and stock status.

Statuses follow our pharmacy SOPs and reflect the stock count of October 23, 2026: near expiry means expiring within 3 months, and reorder means fewer than 2 units on the shelf. All data is synthetic and was created for this prototype.
